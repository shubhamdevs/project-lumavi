Section 1: Product Overview

1.1 Vision Statement

Lumavi is a unified Ai-powered content marketing platform that compresses the entire creative campaign lifecycle, from brand brief to published advertisement, into a single intelligent workspace. It exists to give marketing teams the creative power of a full-service agency without the cost, the fragmentation, or the time.

1.2 Product Philosophy

Every decision made in building Lumavi is governed by five core principles. These are not aspirational values. They are design constraints that every feature must be evaluated against before it ships.

Continuity over context switching: A user should never need to leave Lumavi to complete a creative workflow. Every tool, every integration, every output lives inside one surface. The moment a user has to open another application to finish a task, Lumavi has failed that workflow.

Brand as the operating system: Every generation, every asset, every published piece of content is an expression of a brand. Brand guidelines are not a setup step. they are the engine running underneath every interaction. The platform enforeces brand consistency without the user having to ask for it.

Intelligence compounds over time: The longer a brand used Lumavi, the smarter it gets about that brand. Past performance, creative patterns, audience responses, and brand evolution all feed back into future generation and recommendations. A brand that has used Lumavi for one year should have a fundamentally more powerful experience than one that started yesterday.

Creation should feel like thinking: The fastest path from idea to asset should require no technical skill. A user should be able to describe what they are thinking in plain language and receive a production-ready output. Complexity is abstracted into the platform, not delegated to the user.

Publishing is part of creation: An asset that is not published has no value. The creative workflow does not end at export. It ends when the content is live in front of an audience. Lumavi owns that entire chain.

1.3 Target Users

Lumavi serves distinct user types, each with different needs, skill levels, and success metrics. The platform must serve all of them without compromising on any.

The Brand Marketer, typically works in-house at an SMB or mid-market company. Manages all content creation with a small team or alone. Needs speed, consistency, and the ability to produce agency-quality work without agency budgets. Measures success by campaign output volume, brand consistency and ad performance.

The Creative Director, responsible for brand vision and creative quality. May not generate content themselves but reviews, approves, and directs the creative output of their team. Needs visibility into everything being produced, strong brand control tools, and the ability to set guardrails that the rest of the team works within.

The Performance Marketer, focused on paid advertising outcomes. Cares deeply about ad format compliance, targeting alignment, and the connection between creative decisions and performance metrics. needs direct publishing capability. A/B variant generation, and performance analytics tied back to specific creative choices.

The Agency Producer, manages multiple brand client simultaneously. Need isolated workspaces per client, the ability to move fast across different brand fuidelines, and output vloume that justifies the platform cost acrosss a client protfolio. Measures success by how much proudction time Lumave saves per client.

The Content Creator, an individual creator or freelancer producing content for one or multiple brands. May not have formal design training but has strong creative instincts. Nees an intuitive, low-friction interface where creative vision translates directly into output without technical barriers.

The Platform Administrator, the technical owner of the Lumavi account within an organization. Manages user access, billing, integrations, and security settings, Not a day-to-day content creator but needs comprehensive controls and visibility into platform usage across the team.

1.5 Platform Scope

Lumavi operate across six functional domains. Every feature in this PRD lives within one or more of these domains.

Creation - The primary domain of Lumavi. Encompasses every modality through which content is brought into existence on the platform. This includes:

  • AI image Generation: Producing static visual assets from text prompts, reference images, or brand-guided inputs across multiple styles, formats and aspect ratios.
  • AI Video Generation: Producing short-form video content from prompts, image sequences, or storyboard structures with control over pacing, transitions, and visual style
  • AI Storyboarding: Writing campaign narratives, breaking them into scenes, generation per-scene visual and copy outputs, and assembling them into cohesive video campaigns.
  • Canvas and Story Builder: A visual workspace where users arrange uploaded photos and generated assets into narrative sequences, enriched with AI-matched or AI-generated audio
  • AI Copywriting : Generating headlines, captions, ad copy, voiceover scripts, and long-form campaign narratives guided by brand voice profiles.
  • Template-Based Creation: Using pre-built or community-contributed campaign structures as starting points for new creative work.
  • Multi-Format Adaptation: Automatically reformatting a single creative asset into every platform-required specification without manual resizing or redesign.

Curation - Organizing, searching, versioning, and managing all creative assets.

Collaboration - Enabling teams to review, comment, approve, and iterate on creative work together.

Intelligence- Learning from brand guidelines, past performance and content trends to improve every generation.

Distribution - Publishing content directly to social and ad platforms from within the workspace.

Measurement - Tracking performance across all published content and feeding insights back into the creative layer.

1.6 Design Principles for the User Interface

These Principles apply to every screen, every interaction, and every piece of UI in the platform.

Progressive disclosure. Simple tasks look simple. Advanced controls hould exist but should not crowd the primary interface. A first-time user and a power user should both feel at home on the same screen.

Generation should always feel fast. Even when generation takes time in the background, the interface should feel responsive. Progress feedback, optimistic UI states, and background processing keep the user engaged and informed

Every action should be reversible where possible. Destructive actions require confirmation. Generated assets are never permanently deleted without explicit user intent. Version history protects against accidental loss.

Consistency over cleverness. Interaction patterns should be predictable. A user who learns how one generation flow works should instinctively understand how every other generation flow works.
